## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the average price for Spanish Property Market Guide 2026?

Prices vary significantly by location and property type. Apartments start from €150,000, while luxury villas can exceed €5 million. [Browse current listings](https://propertylist.es/) for real-time pricing.

### Can foreigners buy property in Spain?

Yes! Spain has no restrictions on foreign property ownership. Non-EU citizens may qualify for the Golden Visa program with investments of €500,000 or more.

### What additional costs should I expect?

Budget for approximately 10-12% on top of the purchase price for taxes (ITP or VAT), notary fees, registry fees, and legal costs.

### How long does the buying process take?

Typically 2-3 months from offer acceptance to completion, assuming all documentation is in order.
